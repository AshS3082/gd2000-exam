## 1. Chess with Hidden Information

1a. Describe a variant of chess with hidden information. You should provide
sufficient information that two people could play your variant.

A chess variant with hidden information could include blindness to opponent's pieces and their positions on the board. This would work with a visibility mechanic where you can only see your opponent's pieces in a 2x2 radius from the piece you choose to move. This would mean that strategizing would become more difficult as the player is unable to see all of their opponent's pieces and the possible moves they could execute and the counter movements the player could perform. This would also add a luck factor to the game as the player is unsure as to where their opponent's pieces are. 

1b. How does your variant change the strategy of chess.

The strategy of chess includes the fact that a player can see their opponents pieces at all times and is able to calculate the possible movements their opponent could make and they make moves with their own pieces based on that. This version would eliminate that and add a luck factor to the game as the player becomes unsure of where their opponents pieces are located.

## 3. The game loop

Pick one of: Counterstrike, Monopoly, Pokemon the trading card game,
Ultimate Chicken Horse, Starcraft, Mario Kart

Describe its game loop: what the player does, what the game updates in
response, and what feedback the player gets. Then remove one step of the loop
and describe what breaks.

Game: Ultimate Chicken Horse

The player first chooses an item from the box that they want to place in the environment. After choosing their item the player chooses where they want to place their object in the map. The game updates by placing that object in the desired spot and running the game with the item in place and the player(s) have to reach the goal. The player(s) either reaches the goal or dies which then the game either rewards points towards winning the whole game or not if the map was too easy to complete. After the level is complete another choosing stage is introduced where the cycle repeats until a player wins. 

If you were to remove the choosing items stages then the game would lose its driving force. The game is driven by the player(s) being able to choose items to place in the environment to either help or stall other players. Without this then the game would be impossible to complete as the items help the player(s) reach the end goal.
